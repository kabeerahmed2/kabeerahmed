AI-POWERED PLATFORM WORKING PROTOTYPE

FixHub — Smart Home Services

An AI-orchestrated home repair platform solving service discovery and urgency triage across Pakistani urban centers (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad).

The Problem Addressed

Finding trustworthy, nearby technicians for plumbing, electrical, and appliance repairs is chaotic. Users struggle to describe technical issues accurately and lack real-time proximity transparency.

What I Built

Integrated Google Gemini to allow voice and text problem reporting in English and Urdu. The AI diagnoses the fault, estimates urgency and budget, and coordinates with MapTiler GL JS & Google Maps to display verified nearby technicians, real-time messaging, and booking receipts.

React 18 + Vite Node.js + Express Gemini MapTiler GL Google Maps API Firebase
Key Learning: Multimodal prompt design paired with geospatial coordinates transforms raw LLM intelligence into an immediate real-world utility.
