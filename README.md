# Kabeer Ahmed — Cinematic Glassmorphic Portfolio Website

A luxury, cinematic personal portfolio website for **Kabeer Ahmed** (Software Engineering Student, AI Automation & Prompt Engineer, Web Developer, Creative Technologist, and Founder of Kabeer Tech Hub).

---

## 💎 Design & Art Direction

- **Cinematic Continuous Video Background**: A fixed, full-viewport background video (`assets/portfolio.mp4`) that plays uninterrupted across the entire page lifecycle without restarting on scroll.
- **Atmospheric Charcoal & Amber Grading**:
  - Deep Charcoal: `#0B0B0A`
  - Near-Black: `#11110F`
  - Warm Ivory: `#F4F0E7`
  - Champagne Gold: `#D6B477`
  - Soft Amber: `#E6B96D`
  - Muted Bronze: `#9B7950`
- **Glassmorphism Content System**:
  - Translucent panels floating smoothly over the moving video (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(115%)`).
  - Specular gradient borders with light simulation from above (`border-top: 1px solid rgba(244, 240, 231, 0.28)`).
  - Subtle mouse parallax shifting the ambient specular highlights across cards.
- **Authentic Portrait & Golden Halo**:
  - Real portrait of Kabeer Ahmed surrounded by a distinctive champagne-gold glowing halo and subtle animated orbital rings.
  - Floating technical UI badges highlighting core domains.
