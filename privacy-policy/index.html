Introduction & Scope

02

Information We Collect

We collect information to facilitate technical communication, scope client engineering projects, and ensure the optimal performance of our website. The categories of information collected include:

Contact & Client Data

Name, email address, WhatsApp / phone number, company name, project specifications, and query text submitted voluntarily via our contact form, consultation scheduler, or AI chat assistant widget.

Usage & Telemetry Data

Browser type and version, operating system, hardware device class, IP address, referral URLs, pages visited, time spent per view, interaction clicks, and diagnostic performance metrics.

Cookies & Tracking Tech

Standard session tokens, local storage state (such as chat session identifiers for dialogue continuity), Google Analytics tags (gtag.js), and Google AdSense tracking scripts.

ℹ
Data Minimization Standard: We never request sensitive financial information, banking credentials, or private credentials through public intake forms. Any client API keys or production tokens shared for automation deployment are handled through secure, encrypted communication protocols.
03

How We Use Information

The information collected is used exclusively for legitimate business, engineering, and operational purposes:

  • Project Evaluation & Consultation: Reviewing client requirements, preparing architectural proposals, generating service quotes, and maintaining direct communication via email or WhatsApp.
  • Custom Solution Deployment: Configuring, testing, and deploying agreed-upon AI automations (e.g., n8n workflows, CRM webhooks, WhatsApp bots, and web applications).
  • System Diagnostics & Security: Monitoring website health, identifying broken links or rendering bugs, preventing malicious bot submissions, and safeguarding server endpoints against unauthorized access.
  • Analytics & Site Evolution: Assessing traffic patterns through Google Analytics to optimize portfolio presentation, case study readability, and video walkthrough streaming performance.
04

Third-Party Services & Subprocessors

To maintain high uptime, global performance, and modern automation features, we partner with industry-standard third-party providers. Each provider processes telemetry or operational data under their independent privacy frameworks:

  • Hosting & Edge Delivery: Netlify, Inc. provides edge hosting, global CDN caching, automated SSL/TLS termination, and secure static form submission processing.

05

Data Protection, Storage & Zero-Sale Guarantee

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized disclosure, interception, or destruction:

  • End-to-End Transport Encryption: All communications between your browser and kabeerahmed1.netlify.app are encrypted using modern 256-bit SSL/TLS protocol certificates.
  • Zero-Sale Policy: We explicitly guarantee that Kabeer Tech Hub does not sell, lease, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third-party data brokers, advertising aggregators, or external companies under any circumstances.
  • Retention Schedules: Inactive consultation inquiries and form submissions are routinely audited and pruned from active notification logs once project discussions conclude.
06

User Rights & Privacy Controls

Regardless of your geographic location, we respect your rights to control your personal data in accordance with international best practices and applicable laws:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request a confirmation and copy of any personal contact data we hold concerning your inquiries.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You have the right to request the immediate deletion and scrubbing of your contact history from our messaging records.
  • Right to Rectification: If your submitted email or project contact details change, you may request an immediate update.
  • Cookie & Tracking Opt-Out: You can configure your browser to reject cookies or utilize privacy extensions (such as Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on) to prevent tracking telemetry.
